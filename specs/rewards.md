# Rewards

Rules for how rewards are paid are in architecture.md (Rewards). This spec holds the numbers.

## Reward per difficulty

| Difficulty | Total XP | Total gold |
| ---------- | -------- | ---------- |
| E          | 10       | 5          |
| D          | 20       | 10         |
| C          | 35       | 15         |
| B          | 50       | 25         |
| A          | 75       | 35         |
| S          | 100      | 50         |

## Level curve

- A new character starts at level 1 with 0 XP
- Level n (2 and up) needs 50 × n² total XP, so level 2 is at 200 XP
